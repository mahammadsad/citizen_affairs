## Issued documents and uploaded files

DigiLocker can show documents fetched directly from a participating issuer. These appear in the issued-documents area. A file that you upload yourself is different from a document delivered by an issuer, so check which type you are sharing.

## Step-by-step guide

1. Open the official DigiLocker website or the official mobile app linked from it.
2. Register or sign in and complete the mobile verification requested on screen.
3. Open the section for browsing or searching documents.
4. Choose the relevant category and issuing organization.
5. Select the document type and enter the record details requested by the issuer.
6. Review the consent message, then request the document.
7. Open the issued-documents section to view, download, or share the fetched record using the available controls.

## Sharing and verification

Share documents only with an organization or person you recognize. Before sharing, confirm which document and which details are necessary. A recipient can use DigiLocker's official verification service to scan the secure QR code on a DigiLocker document.

## Troubleshooting

If a record cannot be fetched, check the spelling and numbers against the original record. Availability depends on the issuing authority having supplied the document through DigiLocker. The issuer remains responsible for the underlying record.

Interface labels and supported issuers can change. Verify the latest sign-in, document-fetching, and sharing process on the official DigiLocker portal before acting.

The **RRB NTPC UG CBT 1 Result 2026** under **CEN 07/2025** is being published region-wise by the Railway Recruitment Boards. Candidates who appeared in the Undergraduate NTPC CBT-1 should check the official RRB through which they applied and search their roll number in the shortlisted-candidates PDF.

Official RRB pages are also publishing **cut-off marks for candidates shortlisted for CBT-2**. Because each regional RRB maintains its own notice page, the result link can appear at slightly different times across zones.

## How to check RRB NTPC UG CBT 1 Result 2026

Follow these steps instead of opening a random result link shared on social media:

1. Open the **official website of the RRB through which you applied**.
2. Find the notice for **CEN 07/2025 — NTPC Undergraduate — CBT-1 Result / Candidates Shortlisted for CBT-2**.
3. Open the result PDF.
4. On a computer, press **Ctrl + F**. On a phone, use the PDF search icon.
5. Enter your **roll number** exactly as shown on your e-call letter.
6. If the roll number appears, you have been provisionally shortlisted for CBT-2.
7. Save the official PDF and then check the separate cut-off notice and scorecard link when available.

Do not search only by name unless an official PDF specifically provides names. The shortlist is normally easiest to check by roll number.

## Result PDF, cut-off and scorecard: what is the difference?

These three items are often mixed up, but they answer different questions.

| Document | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Result / shortlist PDF** | Whether your roll number has been shortlisted for CBT-2 |
| **Cut-off notice** | The cut-off marks for the relevant RRB, post/category and reservation category |
| **Scorecard** | Your individual marks/normalised score and candidate-specific details after login |

A cut-off from another RRB should not be used as your final benchmark. Vacancy distribution and category-wise shortlisting can differ by RRB.

## RRB NTPC UG Scorecard 2026: when will it open?

The source article supplied to Citizen Affairs and other current result reporting state that the individual scorecard/login is expected to open on **26 August 2026 at 5:00 PM**.

At our verification check on **25 August 2026**, we had not independently located that exact activation time on an indexed official RRB page. For that reason, treat the time as **provisional until the link appears on your official RRB website or the RRB candidate portal**.

When the scorecard link goes live:

- enter credentials only on an official RRB/Railway domain;
- do not share OTPs, passwords or registration details with agents;
- save a PDF or screenshot of your scorecard for your own record;
- compare your marks with the cut-off published by your own RRB and category.

## RRB NTPC UG Cut Off 2026

Official RRB pages are publishing cut-off marks along with the CBT-1 result process. The cut-off is **not one single all-India number**.

It can vary according to:

- RRB/region;
- notified post/category number;
- UR, OBC, SC, ST, EWS and other applicable reservation categories;
- the shortlisting requirement for CBT-2.

So, if you applied through RRB Kolkata, use the Kolkata cut-off notice; if you applied through Malda, Siliguri or another RRB, use that RRB's own notice.

## What happens after CBT-1?

Candidates shortlisted in CBT-1 move to the **second-stage Computer Based Test (CBT-2)**.

The Ministry of Railways recruitment calendar lists **17 September 2026** as the **tentative** CBT-2 date for CEN 07/2025. It also shows a later third-stage skill-test window for applicable posts. Because the CBT-2 date is marked tentative, candidates should not make non-refundable travel arrangements only from a social-media post or coaching-site update.

For the CBT-2 pattern, city-intimation timing, e-call letter and Aadhaar instructions, read our separate guide: [RRB NTPC UG CBT-2 Exam Date 2026: 17 September, city slip and e-call letter](/articles/rrb-ntpc-ug-cbt-2-exam-date-17-september-2026/).

## What to do if your roll number is shortlisted

If your roll number appears in the official PDF:

1. Save the result PDF.
2. Check your RRB/category cut-off.
3. Download your scorecard when the official login becomes active.
4. Start CBT-2 revision immediately instead of waiting for the scorecard.
5. Keep your registration details and Aadhaar information ready.
6. Watch only official RRB pages for city intimation and e-call letter links.

Shortlisting for CBT-2 is **not final selection**. Later stages still apply according to the notified post.

## If your roll number is not in the PDF

First make sure you are checking the correct RRB and the correct CEN number. Also check whether your regional RRB has actually published its current PDF.

Do not pay anyone who claims they can add a roll number to the result, change marks or guarantee railway selection. Genuine RRB recruitment is based on the notified process.

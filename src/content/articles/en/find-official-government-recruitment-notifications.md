## Start with the recruiting organization

Government recruitment is published by many different commissions, departments, public bodies, and state agencies. There is no safe shortcut that replaces the original notice. First identify the organization named in the claim, then navigate to that organization's official website.

## Step-by-step verification

1. Find the recruitment, advertisement, notice, or careers section on the official website.
2. Locate the notice by its advertisement number, title, and publication date.
3. Download or open the official notice and read it completely.
4. Confirm the post name, eligibility, age rules, selection method, important dates, fee instructions, and reservation details as applicable.
5. Follow the application link from the official notice or the organization's official website.
6. Check the domain again before creating an account, uploading documents, or making any payment.
7. Save the notice and application acknowledgement for your records.

## Cross-checking useful portals

The National Career Service portal can help jobseekers discover opportunities and publishes fraud warnings. Recruitment bodies such as UPSC and SSC maintain their own notification and application sections. A listing on an information site should always be checked against the organization that issued the notice.

## Warning signs

Be cautious when a message promises guaranteed selection, demands payment to a personal account, uses a shortened or misspelled link, asks for an OTP, or refuses to provide an official notice. NCS states that its services are free and warns about entities falsely claiming an association with it.

Recruitment rules and application systems change. Verify every current detail in the original official notification before applying.

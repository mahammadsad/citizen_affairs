## What the official portal can show

The National Health Authority provides a beneficiary portal for searching eligible records and accessing card-related services. The options displayed can differ by state and by the stage of beneficiary verification.

This article does not decide eligibility. The official beneficiary database, applicable scheme rules, and authorized authorities provide the final result.

## Step-by-step guide

1. Open the official NHA beneficiary portal using the official link on this page.
2. Enter your mobile number and complete OTP verification.
3. Select the state and scheme details requested on screen.
4. Search using one of the identity or family-reference options made available by the portal.
5. Match the result carefully with your own family details. Do not select a record only because the name looks similar.
6. Open the beneficiary record to review the status of verification or card availability.
7. If e-KYC or another step is pending, follow only the instructions shown on the official portal or given at an authorized centre.

## If no record appears

Check spelling, state, district, and the selected search method. A missing result does not by itself prove eligibility or ineligibility. State implementation and database records may vary, so use the official portal's support information for clarification.

## Protect your information

Do not pay an unknown person merely to check status, and never disclose an OTP over a call or chat. Confirm that the website address is `beneficiary.nha.gov.in` or another portal linked directly from `pmjay.gov.in`. Verify current eligibility and card instructions on the official portal before acting.

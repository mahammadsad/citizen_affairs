## Before you begin

This guide is for a request that has already been submitted. Keep the acknowledgement or receipt from the online service or enrolment centre. The reference number printed on it helps the official system find your request.

Never share an OTP, Aadhaar number, or scanned identity document with someone who contacts you unexpectedly. UIDAI is the final authority for the status and any action you must take.

## Step-by-step guide

1. Open the official myAadhaar portal from the official links section on this page.
2. Choose the option for checking enrolment or update status. The wording can vary according to the service you used.
3. Enter the reference details requested on the screen. These may include an Enrolment ID, Service Request Number, or Update Request Number.
4. Complete the security check or OTP verification if the official portal asks for it.
5. Review the status displayed on screen. Save a screenshot or note the reference number for your records.
6. If the portal asks for another action, confirm that the instruction appears on the UIDAI domain before proceeding.

## Understanding the result

A request may be shown as submitted, under review, completed, or rejected. The exact labels can change as UIDAI updates its portal. If completed, use the official download or Aadhaar service offered by UIDAI. If rejected, use the reason shown on the portal to decide whether a new request is necessary.

## Safety reminder

Search advertisements and unofficial agents can imitate public-service pages. Check that the address belongs to `uidai.gov.in` or `myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in`. Always verify the latest process and document requirements on the official UIDAI portal before acting.

The **India Post GDS Recruitment 2026 Schedule-II** page is now active on the Department of Posts' official GDS portal. The portal currently identifies the recruitment cycle as **Gramin Dak Sevak (GDS) Online Engagement Schedule-II, July-2026** and confirms the registration, application and correction schedule.

For candidates, the most important change since our first report is simple: this is no longer only a circulated notice-page update. The **official India Post portal itself is now displaying Schedule-II as the active notification**. Registration begins on **31 August 2026**, while online applications begin on **2 September 2026**.

> **Verification note:** Citizen Affairs is treating the dates and active Schedule-II status as confirmed from the official portal. We are **not publishing an estimated all-India vacancy total as an official number**. India Post provides separate **Vacant Posts** and **Annexures** sections on the portal, and candidates should use those sections for the current division-wise vacancy position.

## Important dates for GDS Schedule-II 2026

The official India Post GDS portal currently shows the following schedule:

| Activity | Date |
| --- | --- |
| One-Time Registration | **31 August 2026 to 19 September 2026, up to 17:00 hrs** |
| Online application submission and fee payment | **2 September 2026 to 21 September 2026, up to 17:00 hrs** |
| Edit/Correction Window | **23 September 2026 to 24 September 2026, up to 17:00 hrs** |

If you plan to apply, do not wait until the final hours. Complete registration first, then submit the application during the separate application window.

## India Post GDS vacancy 2026: do not rely on an estimated total

Several recruitment pages and social posts may quote a large national vacancy number for Schedule-II. Citizen Affairs is not presenting those estimates as an official total.

The official GDS portal has dedicated **Vacant Posts** and **Annexures** sections. The safer approach is to check the vacancy position there when India Post makes the relevant list available for your Circle or Division.

This matters because a candidate's useful information is not only the national total. The **Circle, Division, office, post preference and applicable local-language requirement** can affect which vacancy is actually relevant to an applicant.

## What the Schedule-II notice page already confirms

Citizen Affairs previously published the supplied Schedule-II notice page while the main India Post portal was still showing the older recruitment cycle. That notice page identifies **Notification No. 17-12/2026-GDS dated 20 August 2026** and refers to BPM, ABPM and Dak Sevak engagement.

It also says that **only one registration per candidate is permitted**. Multiple or duplicate registrations/applications are prohibited. If a candidate notices a mistake after registration or submission, the notice directs the candidate to use the official correction facility rather than register again.

The supplied notice page further states that candidates do not need to upload supporting documents with the application form at that stage, but they must upload a **recent photograph and signature** in the prescribed manner.

## Eligibility, fee and detailed conditions: verify the active notification before acting

The live India Post portal now exposes a **Notification-English** control along with separate registration, application, fee and correction instructions. Candidates should open the current notification from the portal and confirm that its heading says **Schedule-II, July-2026** before relying on detailed eligibility, fee, reservation, TRCA or document conditions.

This check is especially important because India Post uses a continuously updated GDS portal. A saved or cached PDF from an earlier recruitment cycle can look similar while containing different dates or annexures.

Citizen Affairs will re-check the portal when registration opens on **31 August 2026** and update this page if India Post publishes additional vacancy or instruction details.

## How to apply for India Post GDS Recruitment 2026

Use only the Department of Posts' official online system. The practical sequence is:

1. Open the **official India Post GDS Online Engagement portal**.
2. Read the active Schedule-II notification and the registration instructions.
3. Complete One-Time Registration from **31 August 2026**.
4. Submit the online application from **2 September 2026** after checking the eligible Division/post preferences shown by India Post.
5. Follow the official fee instructions if a fee applies to you.
6. Upload the prescribed recent photograph and signature.
7. Review the application carefully before final submission.
8. If a permitted correction is needed, use the **23-24 September 2026** edit/correction window instead of creating a duplicate registration.

## Beware of fake GDS recruitment messages

India Post's active portal carries a security notice telling candidates not to trust communications from unauthorised or suspicious email IDs. The Department says official GDS communications use authorised India Post domains.

Never share an OTP, password or payment credential with a person claiming they can secure a GDS post.

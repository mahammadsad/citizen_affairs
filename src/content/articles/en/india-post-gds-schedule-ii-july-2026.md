The Department of Posts notice page uploaded to Citizen Affairs identifies **Notification No. 17-12/2026-GDS dated 20 August 2026** and the recruitment cycle **“Gramin Dak Sevak (GDS) Online Engagement Schedule-II, July-2026.”** It covers engagement for **Branch Postmaster (BPM), Assistant Branch Postmaster (ABPM) and Dak Sevak** posts in different Department of Posts offices.

> **Verification note:** the uploaded file is **page 2 of a 39-page notice**. When Citizen Affairs checked the official India Post GDS portal on 22 August 2026, the public portal view available to us was still showing Schedule-I, January-2026. For that reason, this update is being published as **under verification** until the complete Schedule-II notification is available on the official portal. Vacancy totals, detailed eligibility, fee categories and other conditions should not be inferred from this single page.

## Important dates shown in the notice

| Activity | Schedule |
| --- | --- |
| Online One-Time Registration | 31 August 2026 to 19 September 2026, up to 17:00 hrs |
| Submission of online applications and fee payment | 2 September 2026 to 21 September 2026, up to 17:00 hrs |
| Corrections in registration/application forms, including online fee payment if applicable | 23 September 2026 to 24 September 2026, up to 17:00 hrs |

## What candidates should know from this page

The notice says that only **one registration per candidate** is permitted. Multiple or duplicate registrations/applications are prohibited, and a duplicate application can lead to cancellation of candidature or other action under the GDS rules.

Candidates are not required to upload supporting documents along with the application form on this page's instructions. However, they **must upload a recent photograph and signature** in the prescribed manner. The notice also advises candidates to review all registration and application details carefully before final submission.

If a mistake is noticed after registration or application submission, candidates should **not register again**. A two-day edit/correction window is provided after the application closing date.

## What is not confirmed from the uploaded page

This single page does **not** provide the complete state/circle-wise vacancy list, full eligibility conditions, age rules, fee categories, reservation conditions, selection process or all documents required at later stages. These details should be checked only after the complete Schedule-II notification and annexures are available from the Department of Posts.

Do not rely on vacancy numbers circulating on social media or third-party job websites unless the figure can be matched to the official Annexure-I.

Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have issued a schedule notice for the **second-stage Computer Based Test (CBT-II)** under **CEN 07/2025, Non-Technical Popular Categories (Under Graduate)**. The notice supplied to Citizen Affairs is dated **24 August 2026** and gives **17 September 2026** as the tentative CBT-II date.

> **Verification note:** Citizen Affairs has the supplied image of the RRB notice and has independently confirmed the official CEN 07/2025 recruitment page and RRB candidate portal. At the time of checking on 24 August 2026, however, we could not independently locate the exact 24 August CBT-II schedule PDF on the official RRB pages indexed and available to us. This article is therefore published as **under verification**. Candidates should confirm the schedule on their regional RRB website before making travel or examination arrangements.

## RRB NTPC UG CBT-2 exam date

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Recruitment | CEN 07/2025 — NTPC (Under Graduate) |
| Exam stage | Computer Based Test-II (CBT-II) |
| Tentative exam date | **17 September 2026** |
| Exam City & Date link | 10 days before the exam date |
| SC/ST Travel Authority | To be made available with the city/date link for eligible candidates |
| E-call letter | 4 days before the exam date shown in the city/date intimation |

The notice describes the schedule as **tentative**, which means candidates should continue checking official RRB updates in case the date is revised.

## When will the Exam City & Date link be available?

According to the notice, the link for viewing the **Exam City & Date** will be made live **10 days before the examination date** on the official websites of the Railway Recruitment Boards.

The same link is also expected to provide the **Travel Authority for eligible SC/ST candidates**.

Candidates should use only their regional RRB's official website or the official RRB candidate portal and should avoid links circulated through unofficial social-media posts, messaging groups or third-party job sites.

## When will the CBT-2 e-call letter be released?

The notice says downloading of the **e-call letter** will begin **4 days before the exam date mentioned in the Exam City & Date intimation link**.

Candidates should check the city/date information first and then use the official RRB link to download the call letter when it becomes active.

## Aadhaar biometric authentication at the exam centre

The notice states that **Aadhaar-linked biometric authentication** will be carried out at the examination centre before candidates are allowed to enter the exam hall.

Candidates are instructed to bring either:

- their **original Aadhaar card**, or
- a **printout of e-verified Aadhaar**.

The notice also advises candidates who have not already completed Aadhaar-based identification verification to log in with their credentials through the official RRB candidate portal at **rrbapply.gov.in** so that entry and registration at the exam centre can proceed smoothly.

## Keep Aadhaar unlocked and details updated

RRB has specifically advised candidates to make sure their Aadhaar remains in an **unlocked condition** in the UIDAI system before going to the examination centre.

The notice further asks candidates to ensure that Aadhaar details are updated correctly, including the candidate's name, date of birth as per the Class 10 certificate, latest photograph, biometrics where required, address and father's name.

Because Aadhaar settings and update procedures are handled by UIDAI, candidates should use only official UIDAI services for any Aadhaar change or unlock action.

## What candidates should do now

1. Keep checking the official website of the RRB through which you applied.
2. Watch for the Exam City & Date link around the period specified in the official notice.
3. Download the e-call letter only from the official RRB link when it becomes active.
4. Keep your original Aadhaar card or e-verified Aadhaar printout ready for the exam day.
5. Make sure Aadhaar is not locked in the UIDAI system before travelling to the examination centre.
6. Recheck the final reporting time, venue and candidate instructions printed on the e-call letter.

## Beware of fake recruitment claims and touts

The notice reminds candidates to rely only on **official RRB websites** for recruitment updates. It also warns against touts or agents who claim they can secure a railway job in exchange for money or influence.

RRB selections are based on the notified examination and recruitment process. Candidates should not pay any unofficial person for promises of appointment or preferential selection.

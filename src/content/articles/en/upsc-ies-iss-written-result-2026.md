The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has released the Written Result and Written Result (with name) for the Indian Economic Service - Indian Statistical Service (IES/ISS) Examination, 2026. Candidates who appeared for the written examination can now verify their qualification status on the official UPSC portal.

## How to Check and Download UPSC IES/ISS Result PDFs

Candidates can access their results directly through the official commission channels:

1. Visit the Union Public Service Commission home portal at [Welcome to UPSC | UPSC](https://www.upsc.gov.in/).
2. Locate the "What's New" section on the homepage.
3. Click on the links for "Written Result" or "Written Result (with name)" for the Indian Economic Service - Indian Statistical Service Examination, 2026.
4. Alternatively, view updates on the [Active Examinations | UPSC](https://www.upsc.gov.in/examinations/active-exams) page.

## Understanding Roll Number and Name-Wise Result Lists

UPSC issues results in two primary formats for candidate clarity:

- **Written Result:** Lists the roll numbers of all candidates who have successfully qualified in the written examination stage.
- **Written Result (with name):** Provides both candidate names and roll numbers to allow straightforward verification.

*Note on details:* Specific roll numbers, individual candidate scorecards, cut-off marks, and interview/personality test dates are published inside the detailed official PDF notices rather than on the portal summary text.

## Next Steps for Qualified Candidates

Candidates whose names or roll numbers appear on the written result list advance to the Personality Test / Interview stage. UPSC will publish further instructions regarding interview scheduling and document verification on its official web portal.

## How to Verify Authentic UPSC Announcements

To ensure accurate information:

- Always verify notifications directly on the official UPSC website (`https://www.upsc.gov.in/`).
- Download official result PDFs directly from the portal rather than relying on unverified third-party aggregators.

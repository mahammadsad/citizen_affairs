The **SBI Apprentice Result 2026** update is now available on the official State Bank of India Careers website for **Advertisement No. CRPD/APPR/2026-27/07**. SBI currently describes the document as the **List of Provisionally Selected Candidates**.

If you appeared for the SBI Apprentice selection process, use the official SBI Careers page to open the result PDF and check your **roll number or registration number**. Do not depend only on screenshots, Telegram forwards or unofficial lists.

> **Important wording:** Many result websites call this the “SBI Apprentice merit list” or “written exam result”. SBI's own Careers page currently uses the wording **List of Provisionally Selected Candidates**. Citizen Affairs follows the official wording because selection is still subject to the conditions in SBI's advertisement.

## How to check SBI Apprentice Result 2026

1. Open the official **SBI Careers - Current Openings** page.
2. Find **Engagement of Apprentices under the Apprentices Act, 1961**.
3. Confirm the advertisement number is **CRPD/APPR/2026-27/07**.
4. Open **List of Provisionally Selected Candidates**.
5. On a computer, press **Ctrl + F**. On a phone, use the PDF search option.
6. Search your **roll number or registration number** carefully.
7. Save the official PDF for your records.

SBI separately warns candidates about fake selection lists. Its Careers guidance says genuine recruitment results are published through SBI's official careers websites and selection lists use roll numbers or registration numbers rather than candidates' names.

## What does “provisionally selected” mean?

Seeing your number in the PDF is an important result, but do not treat it as an unconditional appointment.

The official Apprentice advertisement says final selection/engagement is subject to:

- verification of eligibility and information submitted in the application;
- meeting the specified local-language requirement, where applicable; and
- being declared medically fit according to SBI's requirement.

Follow any email, SMS or circle-level instruction sent by SBI and keep checking the official Careers page for further directions.

## Local Language Test: who may not need to take it?

SBI says candidates for a State must be able to **read, write, speak and understand** one of the specified local languages for that State.

However, candidates who produce a **10th or 12th standard mark sheet/certificate showing that they studied the specified opted local language** are not required to undergo the language test. Other candidates may have to take the language test at a centre decided by SBI.

## SBI Apprentice West Bengal Result 2026: 460 training seats

For candidates from West Bengal, the official advertisement lists **460 apprentice training seats**. The specified local-language choices are **Bengali or Nepali**.

The category-wise notified West Bengal seat distribution is:

| Category | Seats |
| --- | ---: |
| SC | 105 |
| ST | 23 |
| OBC | 101 |
| EWS | 46 |
| UR | 185 |
| **Total** | **460** |

This state-specific information is useful because SBI's merit process is described as **State-wise and category-wise**, not as one single all-India merit list.

## 7,150 “posts” or training seats?

Search results commonly use the phrase **7,150 posts**, but SBI's official advertisement calls them **training seats of apprentices**.

That difference matters. SBI states that an apprentice is **not an employee of the Bank**, and the apprenticeship is not regular or contract employment with SBI.

The notified training period is **one year**. Apprentices are eligible for a stipend of **₹15,000 per month** during the engagement period and are not entitled to the normal allowances/benefits available to SBI employees.

## What about the 11 July 2026 exam date?

Current result reports state that the online examination was held on **11 July 2026**. SBI's original May advertisement had only stated that the online examination was tentatively planned for July 2026.

For the result itself, the stronger verification is SBI's current Careers page, which now shows the provisional-selection list under the correct advertisement number.

## SBI Apprentice wait list

The official advertisement says SBI will maintain a **State-category-wise wait list for one year from the result date or until the next apprentice engagement exercise, whichever is earlier**. Candidates can be released from the wait list against non-joining, subject to availability of qualified candidates.

So, even if your number is not in the first published list, follow only later official SBI updates rather than paying anyone who claims they can alter the result.

## Important official links

Use these official destinations:

- **SBI Careers - Current Openings:** find CRPD/APPR/2026-27/07 and open the current selection-list PDF.
- **SBI Apprentice 2026 Advertisement:** use it for seats, stipend, local-language rules, selection conditions and wait-list rules.
- **SBI Careers homepage:** use it to cross-check any selection message or suspicious link.

## What should selected candidates keep ready?

Do not rely on an unofficial document checklist as if SBI has issued it for this exact result stage. As a practical step, keep your application details, photo ID, age/qualification proof, category certificate if applicable, and the **10th/12th certificate showing the specified local language** ready. Follow the exact checklist sent by SBI or your allotted circle if it differs.

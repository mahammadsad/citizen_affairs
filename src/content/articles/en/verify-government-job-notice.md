## Check the recruiting authority first

Open the department or recruiting body's official website yourself. Avoid applying through a link copied from an unknown message, advertisement or social-media post.

## Match the important details

Compare the post name, vacancy count, qualification, age limit, application dates, fee and selection process with the official notification PDF.

## Before submitting an application

- Confirm that the web address belongs to the official authority.
- Download and save the official notification.
- Read correction and deadline-extension notices.
- Never share an OTP, password or payment details with an unofficial helper.

## Overview of UPSC NDA 1 2026 Written Examination Result

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has officially announced the publication of the **Written Result (with name)** for the **National Defence Academy and Naval Academy Examination (I), 2026**. Candidates who appeared for the written exam can now access the document directly through the official [UPSC Homepage](https://www.upsc.gov.in/) under the "What's New" updates section.

This examination serves as the gateway for selection into the Army, Navy, and Air Force wings of the National Defence Academy (NDA) and the Indian Naval Academy Course (INAC). The published document lists qualified candidates by name and roll number, establishing their eligibility for the next evaluation stage conducted by the Services Selection Board (SSB).

## How to Download NDA & NA (I) 2026 Result PDF with Names

To view or download the candidate merit list directly from official government sources, follow these steps:

1. Visit the official portal of the Union Public Service Commission at [upsc.gov.in](https://www.upsc.gov.in/).
2. Navigate to the **What's New** section on the homepage, or check the [UPSC Active Examinations](https://www.upsc.gov.in/examinations/active-exams) tab.
3. Click on the link titled **Written Result (with name): National Defence Academy and Naval Academy Examination (I), 2026**.
4. A PDF document will open in your browser containing the list of successful candidate names and corresponding roll numbers.
5. Save or print the PDF document for future reference during SSB interviews and document verification.

## Mandatory Next Steps for Qualified Candidates

Candidates whose names appear in the written result list must prepare for the second stage of the selection process—the SSB Interview.

* **Portal Registration:** Shortlisted candidates are required to register themselves on the official Join Indian Army recruitment website within the timeline specified in the result notice to receive SSB interview center allotment and call-up letters.
* **Profile Updating:** Candidates who already possess an active account on the military recruitment portal should log in to confirm their email address and contact details.
* **Document Verification:** Prepare original academic mark sheets, age proof certificates, and photo identity proofs for verification during the SSB interview process.

## Important Information and Unknowns

Please note the following details regarding published data:

* **Cut-Off Marks & Marksheets:** Specific individual marks, subject-wise qualifying cut-off scores, and final marksheets are not included in the initial written result notice. As per standard procedure, marksheets will be made available on the UPSC portal after the final selection list is published.
* **SSB Schedule:** Exact SSB interview dates, call-up schedules, and center assignments are finalized separately by military authorities and sent to registered candidates.

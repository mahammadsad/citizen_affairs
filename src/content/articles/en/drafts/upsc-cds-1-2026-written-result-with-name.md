## UPSC CDS 1 2026 Written Result (With Name) Published

*Disclaimer: Citizen Affairs is an independent information platform and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) or any government agency. This document is an unpublished editorial draft intended solely for human review.*

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has released the Written Result (with name) for the Combined Defence Services Examination (I), 2026. Candidates who appeared for the written examination can now verify their qualification status by downloading the official name-wise result document directly from the commission's web portal.

## Overview of CDS 1 2026 Written Examination Result

The Combined Defence Services Examination (I), 2026 is listed under the [UPSC Active Examinations section](https://www.upsc.gov.in/examinations/active-exams). The official written result containing candidate names has been published under the What's New bulletin section on the primary [UPSC official portal](https://www.upsc.gov.in/).

Candidates whose names and roll numbers appear in the qualified list are eligible to proceed to the next selection phase, which includes Intelligence and Personality Tests conducted by the Services Selection Board (SSB).

## How to Check and Download CDS 1 2026 Name-Wise Result PDF

To safely access the official result file, follow these step-by-step instructions:

1. Go to the official Union Public Service Commission website at [upsc.gov.in](https://www.upsc.gov.in/).
2. Locate the **What's New** section on the homepage.
3. Click on the link titled **Written Result (with name): Combined Defence Services Examination (I), 2026**.
4. Open or download the PDF document generated by the portal.
5. Use the search tool (`Ctrl + F` on desktop or the search icon on mobile PDF viewers) to locate your full name or roll number.

## Verification and Security: Avoiding Fake Result Lists

During high-traffic examination result periods, third-party sites and social media groups frequently circulate unverified or altered PDFs. Candidates are strongly advised to adhere to basic safety measures:

- Always confirm that the PDF is downloaded from a URL starting strictly with `https://www.upsc.gov.in/`.
- Avoid sharing personal details or paying money to unauthorized websites claiming to provide individual scorecard access.
- Cross-verify any result announcement with official commission notifications.

## Next Steps for Qualified Candidates

Candidates who successfully clear the written test must prepare for the upcoming SSB interview rounds:

- **SSB Registration:** Candidates must complete any required registration on the official defence recruitment portals (such as Join Indian Army, Join Indian Navy, or IAF candidate portals) as instructed by the ministry and service headquarters.
- **Document Readiness:** Keep original educational certificates, identity proofs, matriculation age proofs, and category certificates organized for physical verification.
- **Uncertain Information Note:** Specific cut-off marks, detailed individual scorecards, and non-qualified candidate marks will be released by UPSC only after the final result of the examination (post-SSB interviews) is concluded.

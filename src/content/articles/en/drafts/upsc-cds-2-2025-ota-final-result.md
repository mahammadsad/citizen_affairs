## Overview of Combined Defence Services Examination (II), 2025 (OTA) Final Result

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has officially published the Final Result for the Combined Defence Services Examination (II), 2025 for the Officers Training Academy (OTA) on August 7, 2026. Candidates who participated in this examination cycle can now verify their selection status through the official portal document released by the commission.

*Disclaimer: This document is an unpublished informational draft prepared for public assistance review. It is not an official government publication and has not been formally approved or confirmed by UPSC.*

## How to Access the UPSC CDS II 2025 OTA Final Result PDF

To check whether your roll number is included in the merit list, follow these standard steps to obtain the official file:

1. Visit the verified [UPSC CDS (II) 2025 OTA Final Result Link](https://www.upsc.gov.in/whats-new/Combined%20Defence%20Services%20Examination%20%28II%29%2C%202025%20%28OTA%29/Final%20Result).
2. Download the official PDF document (file size: 346.7 KB).
3. Open the file on your device and search for your Roll Number or Name.

*Note on Document Details: The complete list of candidate roll numbers and qualified names is contained within the downloadable 346.7 KB PDF document. The exact breakdown of candidate scores and individual ranks is published inside that document rather than listed on the primary web landing page.*

## Official UPSC Helpdesk and Contact Information

If you have queries regarding your result, faced issues downloading the official merit list, or require further assistance, contact the Union Public Service Commission using the official phone lines:

- **UPSC Helpdesk Telephones:** 011-24041001 / 011-40303444
- **Toll-Free RTI Helpline:** 1800-118-711

Ensure you have your examination admit card and roll number available when contacting officials.

## Practical Summary of UPSC CAPF AC 2025 Final Result

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) updated its official portal on **August 07, 2026**, issuing the **Final Result** for the **Central Armed Police Forces (Assistant Commandants) Examination, 2025**. Candidates who participated in the examination process can verify their selection status on the official commission portal.

Candidates should note that individual names and roll numbers of shortlisted candidates are embedded within the official PDF document published by UPSC.

## How to Check and Download the Final Result

To view the result on the official website:
1. Visit the Union Public Service Commission portal at [upsc.gov.in](https://www.upsc.gov.in/whats-new/Central%20Armed%20Police%20Forces%20%28ACs%29%20Examination%2C%202025/Final%20Result).
2. Look under the **"What's New"** section on the homepage.
3. Click on the link titled **"Central Armed Police Forces (ACs) Examination, 2025 - Final Result"** (Hindi language candidates can access the [UPSC Hindi Portal Link](https://www.upsc.gov.in/hi/whats-new/Central%20Armed%20Police%20Forces%20(ACs)%20Examination,%202025/Final%20Result)).
4. Open the PDF to search for your roll number.

*Note on Document Listing*: The official English portal lists the result document size as 138.41 KB, whereas the Hindi version lists a document size of 490.24 KB. The direct list of successful candidate roll numbers is maintained within these PDF documents rather than as plaintext on the webpage body.

## Official UPSC Helpdesk & Contact Information

For queries regarding the CAPF (ACs) 2025 final result, candidates can get in touch with UPSC via official telephone lines:

- **General Helpdesk Numbers**: `011-24041001` / `011-40303444`
- **RTI Helpline (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwBD)**: `1800-118-711`

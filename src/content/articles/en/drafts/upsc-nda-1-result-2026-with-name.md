## Overview of UPSC NDA & NA Examination (I) 2026 Result

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has released the **Written Result (with name): National Defence Academy and Naval Academy Examination (I), 2026** on its portal. The notification is listed under the "What's New" section on the official UPSC website and is categorized under active examinations.  

Candidates who sat for the written entrance test can now download the result document to check whether their name and roll number are included among the qualified candidates.

## How to Check and Download UPSC NDA 1 Name-Wise Result PDF

Candidates can access their written examination results directly from the official portal by following these steps:

1. Go to the official UPSC portal at [upsc.gov.in](https://www.upsc.gov.in/).
2. Navigate to the **What's New** section on the homepage or visit the [Active Examinations](https://www.upsc.gov.in/examinations/active-exams) page.
3. Click on the link titled **Written Result (with name): National Defence Academy and Naval Academy Examination (I), 2026**.
4. Download the PDF file to search for your name or roll number.

## Post-Result Procedures and Next Stages

Candidates who clear the written stage proceed to subsequent rounds in the selection framework, including the Services Selection Board (SSB) interview process.

* **SSB Interview Details:** Specific schedules, instructions, and center allocation details for successful candidates are released through official notifications and defence recruitment portals. These detailed interview schedules were not fully specified in the initial result update.
* **Verifying Official Documents:** Always download result documents and notices from official government domains such as `upsc.gov.in` to avoid fraudulent links or fake recruitment notices.

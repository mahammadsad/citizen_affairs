## Practical Summary: Accessing NDA 1 2026 Written Result

Candidates who appeared for the **National Defence Academy and Naval Academy Examination (I), 2026** can check their status through the official portal update released by the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC). The official site listing for the **Written Result (with Name)** was updated on **10 August 2026**.

To view or verify your result details, visit the official UPSC web portal directly at [UPSC Whats New Portal](https://www.upsc.gov.in/whats-new/National%20Defence%20Academy%20and%20Naval%20Academy%20Examination%20%28I%29%2C%202026/Written%20Result%20%28with%20name%29).

## How to Download NDA 1 2026 Name-Wise Written Result

1. Go to the official website of the Union Public Service Commission at `upsc.gov.in`.
2. Navigate to the **What's New** section on the homepage.
3. Click on the link for **Written Result (with Name): National Defence Academy and Naval Academy Examination (I), 2026**.
4. Open or download the PDF document listed on the page.
5. Use the search tool (`Ctrl + F`) within the PDF viewer to search for your Name or Roll Number.

*Note on Data Availability:* The individual candidate names, roll numbers, cut-off marks, and candidate PDF lists are hosted directly on the UPSC portal. If a document fails to open, verify that you are on the authentic official UPSC website.

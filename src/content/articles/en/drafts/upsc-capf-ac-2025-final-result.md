## Practical Summary
The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has officially updated its portal to release the final result for the Central Armed Police Forces (Assistant Commandants) Examination, 2025. Candidates who appeared for the exam can check their selection status directly through the official [UPSC CAPF AC 2025 Final Result Portal](https://www.upsc.gov.in/whats-new/Central%20Armed%20Police%20Forces%20%28ACs%29%20Examination%2C%202025/Final%20Result).

## Overview of UPSC CAPF AC 2025 Final Result Announcement
On August 7, 2026, the Union Public Service Commission published the final results for the Central Armed Police Forces (ACs) Examination, 2025. This declaration marks the culmination of the selection process for Assistant Commandant positions across central armed police units.

Candidates should note that specific roll numbers, names, and individual marks breakdowns are contained within the downloadable PDF result file linked on the official portal. There is a minor listing discrepancy on the official web page: the English portal indicates a PDF file size of 138.41 KB, whereas the Hindi language version lists the file size as 490.24 KB.

## How to Access and Download the Official Final Result PDF
To check whether your roll number is included in the merit list, follow these steps:

1. Visit the official UPSC final result page via the [UPSC CAPF AC Result Link](https://www.upsc.gov.in/whats-new/Central%20Armed%20Police%20Forces%20%28ACs%29%20Examination%2C%202025/Final%20Result).
2. Click on the document link to download or open the result PDF file.
3. Open the downloaded PDF document and use the search function (Ctrl + F on desktop devices) to search for your Roll Number or Name.
4. Save a digital copy of the merit list for your records.

## Contacting UPSC Helpdesk and Toll-Free Helpline
Candidates seeking assistance, clarification on recruitment proceedings, or guidance regarding their results can reach the Union Public Service Commission official helpdesks through the following channels:

* **Standard Helpdesk Phone Numbers:** 011-24041001 / 011-40303444
* **Toll-Free Helpline (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwBD Candidates):** 1800-118-711

Ensure you keep your candidate registration number and roll number ready when calling the commission's helpdesk.

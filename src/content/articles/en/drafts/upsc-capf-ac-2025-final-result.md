## Overview of UPSC CAPF (ACs) 2025 Final Result

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has officially announced the Final Result for the Central Armed Police Forces (Assistant Commandants) Examination, 2025. Candidates who appeared for the recruitment process can now check their final qualification status directly from the commission's portal.

## How to Download Official CAPF AC 2025 Final Result PDF

To view and verify your merit position in the Central Armed Police Forces (ACs) Examination 2025:

1. Visit the official UPSC portal.
2. Navigate to the **What's New** section.
3. Access the official link for the [UPSC CAPF (ACs) 2025 Final Result Notification](https://www.upsc.gov.in/whats-new/Central%20Armed%20Police%20Forces%20%28ACs%29%20Examination%2C%202025/Final%20Result).
4. Download the result PDF to check candidate roll numbers and selection details.

*Note: The detailed list of selected candidate roll numbers, names, and cutoff details are contained directly inside the official result PDF document hosted on upsc.gov.in.*

## Official UPSC Helpdesk and Inquiry Contacts

If you need official clarification or help regarding your examination result status, contact the UPSC support lines:

- **UPSC Helpdesk Phone Numbers:** 011-24041001 / 011-40303444
- **UPSC RTI Helpline Number:** 1800-118-711

## Verification and Anti-Scam Guidance

Always verify recruitment results and download selection lists exclusively from official websites ending in `.gov.in`. Avoid sharing personal data or relying on unverified third-party files.

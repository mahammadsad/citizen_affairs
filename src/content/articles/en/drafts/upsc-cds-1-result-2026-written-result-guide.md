## Overview of Combined Defence Services Examination (I) 2026 Written Result

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has officially announced the results for the Combined Defence Services Examination (I), 2026. The commission published both the **Written Result** (roll-number list) and the **Written Result (with name)** under the "What's New" portal section. 

Additionally, the Combined Defence Services Examination (I), 2026 remains listed under the Active Examinations status on the commission's official portal. Candidates who sat for the written test can now check whether they have cleared the initial written qualification phase.

## How to Check CDS 1 Written Result on upsc.gov.in

Candidates can access their results directly by following these steps on the official UPSC site:

1. Visit the main Union Public Service Commission homepage at [upsc.gov.in](https://www.upsc.gov.in/).
2. Locate the **What's New** section on the right-hand panel or main dashboard.
3. Click on the link titled **Written Result: Combined Defence Services Examination (I), 2026** to open the roll-number selection PDF.
4. To verify candidate names, select the link titled **Written Result (with name): Combined Defence Services Examination (I), 2026**.
5. Download the PDF and use the search function (Ctrl+F or Command+F) to locate your Roll Number or full Name.

Candidates may also review active status details through the [UPSC Active Examinations portal](https://www.upsc.gov.in/examinations/active-exams).

## Selection Process and Post-Result Steps

Shortlisted candidates from the written examination proceed to the interview round conducted by the Services Selection Board (SSB) of the Ministry of Defence. 

* **SSB Interview Instructions:** Qualified candidates are typically required to register on the designated defence recruitment portals within specified timelines to receive SSB interview center allocations and call letters.
* **Marksheet and Cutoffs:** Individual candidate marks, qualifying cut-off scores, and detailed scorecards are released by UPSC after the complete selection process concludes, as per standard administrative policy.

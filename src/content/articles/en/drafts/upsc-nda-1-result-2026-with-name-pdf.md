## Practical Overview of UPSC NDA & NA (I) 2026 Written Result

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has formally updated its official portal with the listing **"Written Result (with name): National Defence Academy and Naval Academy Examination (I), 2026"**. Candidates who appeared for the written examination can access the name-wise PDF directly from the official website to verify whether they have qualified for the Services Selection Board (SSB) interview stage.

Candidates whose names and roll numbers appear in the written result PDF are eligible to proceed to the next stage of selection conducted by the Ministry of Defence.

## How to Download and Search Your Name in the Written Result PDF

To verify your qualifying status in the written examination without relying on third-party links, follow these steps on the official UPSC platform:

1. Visit the official website of the Union Public Service Commission at [upsc.gov.in](https://www.upsc.gov.in/).
2. Navigate to the **What's New** section on the homepage or open the [Active Examinations](https://www.upsc.gov.in/examinations/active-exams) directory.
3. Click on the link titled **Written Result (with name): National Defence Academy and Naval Academy Examination (I), 2026**.
4. Open or download the PDF document.
5. Use the search function (`Ctrl + F` on Windows or `Cmd + F` on Mac) to search for your full name or assigned roll number.

If your name appears in the published list, you have cleared the written stage of NDA & NA (I) 2026.

## Next Steps After Clearing the NDA 1 Written Examination

Candidates who have qualified in the written examination must complete the following mandatory procedural requirements:

- **SSB Portal Registration:** Qualified candidates must register online on the respective defence service recruitment portals (such as Join Indian Army) within the prescribed timeframe following result publication.
- **Call Up Letter & Selection Centre Allotment:** Registered candidates will receive allotment details for their SSB interview centers, dates, and call-up letters through official candidate portals.
- **Uncertainty Note:** Specific cut-off marks, detailed SSB interview schedules, and individual mark sheets are released by UPSC only after the final result declaration post-SSB interview process.

## Verifying Official Results and Security Precautions

- **Check Portal Authenticity:** Always ensure you download result PDFs directly from the official domain `upsc.gov.in` to guard against fraudulent portals or altered text files.
- **Report Fake Communications:** Beware of unsolicited messages or emails claiming guaranteed SSB selection or requesting payment for physical tests. Official government exam results do not charge fees for candidate list verification.

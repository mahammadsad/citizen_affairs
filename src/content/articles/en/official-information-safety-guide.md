## Use this five-point check

- Is the announcement available on an official department website?
- Does it include a notification number or dated order?
- Are the amount, eligibility and timeline stated in the original document?
- Has the department issued a correction or clarification?
- Does the application link use the authority's official domain?

A news report can help you discover an update, but it should not replace the original government notice. When official confirmation is unavailable, we label the information as under verification instead of presenting it as final.

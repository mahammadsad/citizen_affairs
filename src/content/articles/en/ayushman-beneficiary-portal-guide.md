The National Health Authority's beneficiary portal provides beneficiary-search and related verification or card services where available.

## Safer way to use the portal

1. Type the official address directly in your browser.
2. Select the requested state or scheme details carefully.
3. Use only your own mobile number and OTP.
4. Review the status shown by the portal before taking the next step.

Availability depends on the beneficiary record and the options currently shown on the official portal. Do not pay anyone merely to check a status that you can view yourself.

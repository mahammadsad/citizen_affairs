# Corrections procedure

1. Record the reader report and preserve its article URL and supporting source.
2. Compare the claim with the current primary official source.
3. If factual content changes, create a new article version and repeat required approvals.
4. Record what was incorrect, the corrected information, reason and source.
5. Publish material corrections in the public Corrections log and article history.
6. Withdraw an article if its central claim cannot be made safe and accurate.

Minor spelling or formatting changes that do not change meaning do not need a public correction. Never silently alter a deadline, amount, vacancy, eligibility rule or official link.

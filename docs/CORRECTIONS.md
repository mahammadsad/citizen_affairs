# Corrections procedure

## Ticket and severity model

Every report receives an ID in the form `CA-COR-YYYY-NNN`. Record the article URL, severity, received/acknowledged/resolved timestamps, explanation, before/after text, supporting official source, status, and accountable editor. Material and critical resolved records appear both on the affected article and in the central public ledger.

| Severity | Meaning | Acknowledge target | Resolution/update target |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Critical | Could cause immediate financial, legal, safety, eligibility, or deadline harm; or the central claim is unsupported | 4 hours | Correct/withdraw as soon as verified, target 12 hours |
| Material | Could change a reasonable reader's understanding or action | 1 business day | Target 3 business days |
| Minor | Meaning is not materially changed (grammar, formatting, clarity) | 3 business days | Target 10 business days; public ledger normally not required |

Targets begin only when the email is received and are not guarantees. The owner still needs to appoint a correction owner and complete a real drill; code readiness is not operational proof.

## Email-only intake

The public route prepares an email and does not submit to a web endpoint. Ask for the article URL, disputed statement, proposed correction, and official source. Do not request attachments or unnecessary personal data. Security reports follow root `SECURITY.md`.

1. Record the reader report and preserve its article URL and supporting source.
2. Compare the claim with the current primary official source.
3. If factual content changes, create a new article version and repeat required approvals.
4. Record what was incorrect, the corrected information, reason and source.
5. Publish material corrections in the public Corrections log and article history.
6. Withdraw an article if its central claim cannot be made safe and accurate.

Minor spelling or formatting changes that do not change meaning do not need a public correction. Never silently alter a deadline, amount, vacancy, eligibility rule or official link.

# Beat and source monitoring

This is a monitoring checklist, not proof that daily monitoring or staffing exists. Record a real owner and completed check before using an operational status.

| Beat | Primary source types | Review SLA while active | Immediate triggers |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Government jobs | Recruiting authority notice board, advertisement, corrigendum, application portal | Daily; within 4 hours on deadline day | extension, cancellation, vacancy/fee/eligibility change |
| Exams | Conducting authority notice board, admit-card/result portal, answer-key/counselling notice | Daily during an active stage; weekly otherwise | schedule, centre, answer-key, result or counselling change |
| Schemes | Ministry/department guideline, cabinet/order record, official scheme portal | Every 30 days; weekly during a changed rollout | eligibility, benefit, geography, application or status change |
| Public notices | Issuing authority order/notification and later corrigenda | Daily while current; close at expiry | supersession, cancellation, effective-date change |
| Citizen services | Responsible department portal, service notice, authorised delivery portal | Every 14 days; daily during interruption | domain, fee, document, availability or privacy-flow change |
| Alerts | Issuing authority order and service-status source | Every 4 hours while urgent; daily while important | new order, expiry, escalation, withdrawal or restored service |

## Per-source record

For every monitored source, record: beat, authority, exact listing URL, jurisdiction/geography, source type, named monitor, last successful check, next check, observed newest document/date, access failure, escalation route and notes. A homepage alone is not enough when an exact notice/listing feed exists.

## Check sequence

1. Confirm the domain and issuing authority.
2. Inspect new notices plus corrigenda, extensions, cancellations and withdrawals.
3. Compare notice number, dates and affected geography with the current article/draft.
4. Record `no change`, `candidate found`, `source unreachable` or `escalated`; silence is not success.
5. Create only a private draft candidate. Do not copy a social post or search snippet as evidence.
6. Shorten `nextReviewDate` for active deadlines and alerts; withdraw unsafe central claims.

Thirty distinct Bengali topics and a sustained publishing cadence require real editorial work. This checklist must not be used to fabricate either result.

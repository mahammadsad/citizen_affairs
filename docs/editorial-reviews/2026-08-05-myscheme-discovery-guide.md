# Editorial review: myScheme discovery guide

- **Article:** `src/content/articles/bn/find-government-schemes-with-myscheme.md`
- **Review date:** 2026-08-05
- **Next review:** 2026-09-05
- **Decision:** publish after source and safety corrections
- **Verification state:** `partially-confirmed`
- **Public category:** Welfare Schemes (`projects`)

## Scope

The review checked how myScheme describes scheme discovery, eligibility matching, scheme details, application handoff and user-supplied information. It did not verify the rules of every individual Central, State or Union Territory scheme indexed by the portal.

## Primary sources checked

1. myScheme national platform homepage
2. About myScheme
3. myScheme frequently asked questions
4. myScheme Terms of Use
5. Find Schemes for You flow

All sources are current official pages under `myscheme.gov.in`, operated through the National e-Governance Division and Digital India Corporation under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology.

## Claims confirmed

- myScheme is a national one-stop search and discovery platform for Central, State and Union Territory schemes.
- The scheme finder uses basic demographic, income, social-category and related details to display relevant schemes.
- Dedicated scheme pages provide eligibility, benefits, required documents, FAQs and application-process information.
- The user may be directed to the relevant external application URL.
- Information supplied by the user should be complete and accurate.

## Editorial boundaries

- A displayed match is preliminary discovery, not a final legal eligibility determination.
- Benefit approval, document verification and local implementation remain with the relevant scheme authority.
- The guide does not state that every scheme accepts applications directly inside myScheme.
- A destination opened from myScheme must still be checked against the relevant ministry, department or authorised portal.
- Generic document examples are clearly labelled as examples, not universal requirements.

## Safety additions

- Verify the complete application domain and implementing authority.
- Do not send money to personal UPI IDs or private bank accounts.
- Do not share OTP, bank PIN, card PIN, CVV or account passwords.
- Avoid agents, forwarded links, old videos and cached deadlines.
- Recheck the scheme page on the day of application.

## Publication decision

The article is useful as an evergreen discovery guide and does not advertise a specific benefit or deadline. It may be published as `partially-confirmed` and unfeatured.

It must not be labelled `officially-confirmed`, because no separate human fact-checker profile is registered and the review cannot confirm every scheme record or individual reader's eligibility.

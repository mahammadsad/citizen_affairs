## Material finding

The draft originally described `services.india.gov.in` as the active National Government Services Portal. During review, that address redirected to `https://www.india.gov.in/services`. Government portal notices state that services were migrated to the National Portal and the earlier portal was discontinued from 15 May 2026.

Publishing the original draft would therefore have given readers outdated navigation instructions.

## Corrections made

- Replaced the legacy services portal as the recommended destination with `https://www.india.gov.in/services`.
- Added IGOD as a second official directory for locating government websites.
- Added structured primary-source records with access dates and publishing authority.
- Rewrote the title and summary in clearer Bengali.
- Expanded checks for domains, notices, mobile applications, payments and personal information.
- Added an FAQ explaining the legacy portal redirect.
- Kept the article unfeatured so publication does not displace higher-priority content automatically.

## Verification boundary

The guide explains a verification method and current official directories. It does not certify that every website listed by those directories will remain available or that every service page is current.

No separate human fact checker is registered in the repository. The article must therefore remain `verificationStatus: partially-confirmed`; it must not be labelled `officially-confirmed` until an independent reviewer completes and records that check.

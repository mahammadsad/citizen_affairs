## Scope

The review checked the article's major eligibility, financial-benefit and approval-period claims against current Government of India portals and official releases. The article is a cross-scheme explainer, not an application or eligibility determination.

## Material findings

### PM POSHAN

The previous article only warned that the approval after 31 March 2026 needed another check. The Ministry of Education's Programme Approval Board page now states that the scheme is temporarily continued with the same scope, structure, coverage and cost norms until 30 September 2026 or the date of approval for the next Finance Commission cycle, whichever is earlier.

The article now states that temporary continuation explicitly and does not imply a completed new five-year approval.

### Samagra Shiksha

The earlier five-year approval ended on 31 March 2026. Official January 2026 consultations and 2026-27 planning show that the next phase is being implemented and designed; a July 2026 parliamentary reply also describes continuing ICT and smart-classroom work under Samagra Shiksha.

No complete new multi-year Cabinet/CCEA approval with a final end date and total outlay was located during this review. The article therefore distinguishes current implementation from a confirmed long-term approval.

### Source quality

The previous source list included a non-government explainer from Accountability Initiative. It has been removed. The current source list is limited to Government of India portals and official releases.

## Claims reconfirmed

- Jal Jeevan Mission 2.0 is approved through December 2028.
- PMAY-G is approved for FY 2024-25 through 2028-29 with an additional ceiling of two crore rural houses.
- AB PM-JAY has been expanded to people aged 70 years and above through the Ayushman Vay Vandana Card arrangement.
- NFSA entitlement foodgrain is approved free of cost for five years from 1 January 2024.
- PMJDY's official scheme page states no minimum balance, eligible overdraft up to ₹10,000 and the applicable ₹1 lakh/₹2 lakh RuPay accident cover boundary.
- PM-KISAN provides ₹6,000 per year in three equal instalments to eligible cultivable landholding farmer families, subject to exclusions.
- MGNREGA remains demand-driven and legally guarantees up to 100 days of employment per rural household per financial year.
- Mission Vatsalya, Mission Shakti and Saksham Anganwadi and Poshan 2.0 descriptions match their current official programme pages.

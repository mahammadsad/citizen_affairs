# Weekly newsletter requirements

Status: **not launched**. The public site must not show a signup field until every required decision and test below is complete.

## Owner decisions and evidence

- legal publisher/controller and approved sender identity;
- provider and data-processing agreement, hosting/transfer location and subprocessors;
- exact value proposition and weekly cadence that can be sustained;
- explicit, unticked consent wording and versioning;
- double opt-in and proof-of-consent record;
- retention for unconfirmed, active, unsubscribed, bounced and suppressed addresses;
- one-click unsubscribe, deletion and grievance routes;
- access roles, account recovery, 2FA and breach/incident process;
- privacy-notice and processor disclosure approved for India and intended audience.

## Minimum product behaviour

Use email only for the subscribed briefing. Do not bundle marketing or partner consent, import contacts, infer consent from community membership, expose recipient lists, or require unrelated personal data. Confirmation, unsubscribe and deletion must work without logging into Citizen Affairs.

The first test uses only authorised internal addresses, proves double opt-in, unsubscribe, suppression, deletion/export and failure handling, and records screenshots/timestamps. A provider-free HTML mockup is not a launch. Until approval, retain the current no-form/no-collection state.

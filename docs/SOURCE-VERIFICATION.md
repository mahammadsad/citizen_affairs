# Source verification

## Source order

1. Official notification, order or gazette.
2. Official department/ministry portal.
3. Official press release or government dataset.
4. Secondary material only for context, never as the sole basis for Officially Confirmed facts.

Record the title, authority, URL, document number, publication/access dates, primary/secondary designation and notes. Archive a URL when lawful and useful.

For jobs, verify every vacancy, qualification, age rule, fee, pay detail, deadline, selection step and application link. For schemes, verify authority, benefit, eligibility, limits, required documents, application process, portal and current status.

For exams, distinguish the notification, application, city slip, admit card, answer key, response, result and counselling stage; verify the conducting authority, notice number/date, relevant dates, geography, eligibility/fee summary and exact official action. For notices, verify the issuing authority, notice/effective/expiry dates, affected people and geography, then check later corrigenda, supersession, cancellation or withdrawal. For citizen services, verify the responsible department, service geography, eligibility, documents, fee, processing time, current availability and official portal. For alerts, verify the issuing authority, affected people/geography, effective/expiry dates, urgency and exact order; withdraw or close an alert when the source does.

Use `docs/BEAT_SOURCE_MONITORING.md` to record real source checks and freshness SLAs. A checklist, generated candidate or unchanged homepage is not proof that monitoring occurred.

Never infer eligibility. Public wording should say: “You may be eligible. Confirm the final conditions on the official portal.”

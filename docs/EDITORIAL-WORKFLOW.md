# Editorial workflow

The supported stages are Idea, Assigned, Draft, Submitted, Editorial Review, Changes Requested, Fact-Checking, Verification Failed, Copy Review, Final Review, Approved, Scheduled, Published, Re-verification Due, Updating, Corrected, Closed, Withdrawn and Archived.

## Approval rules

- Every transition needs a written reason and produces a workflow event.
- Approval is tied to one article version.
- Editorial, independent fact-check and final approvals plus a primary source are required for approval/publication.
- An author cannot approve or fact-check their own work.
- Job and Scheme records need their structured detail row.
- Factual/source/detail changes increment the version, clear official confirmation where necessary and invalidate old approvals.
- Publication events reject stale versions and duplicate active requests.
- Deletion is soft and recoverable.

Public verification status is separate from internal workflow status. “Officially Confirmed” is an editorial claim about evidence; “Published” is a delivery state.

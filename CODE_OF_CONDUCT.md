# Community conduct

Participation in Citizen Affairs spaces requires respectful, evidence-led collaboration.

- Treat people with dignity; do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate, or expose private information.
- Critique claims and changes, not a contributor’s identity or motives.
- Use primary evidence for factual disputes and label uncertainty honestly.
- Do not pressure contributors to invent approvals, conceal corrections, weaken safety controls, or misrepresent government affiliation.
- Keep security reports private until a fix is available.

Report conduct concerns privately to **citizenaffairs.in@gmail.com**. The current project owner reviews reports and may remove content or restrict participation to protect readers and contributors. This document does not claim an independent moderation team.
